Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how IvoryOS ("the App"), operated by Wispers of the Past ("we", "us"), collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our photography business management application.

1. Information We Collect

IvoryOS collects your email address, name, and business information to provide photography business management services. We also store client and booking data you enter into the app, including client names, contact details, booking dates, contracts, and invoice information.

2. Google Calendar Integration

With your permission, IvoryOS accesses your Google Calendar to create events from your bookings. We only request permission to add events — we do not read, delete, or modify existing calendar events. We do not store your Google Calendar data on our servers.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (supabase.com) infrastructure. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

4. Payments and Subscriptions

Subscriptions and payments are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment card details. Google's handling of your payment information is governed by the Google Play Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing wispersofthepastprints@gmail.com. We will delete your account and personal data within 30 days of your request, except where we are required by law to retain certain records (for example, financial transaction records).

6. Children's Privacy

IvoryOS is a business tool and is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted at this URL with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, or to exercise your data rights, contact: wispersofthepastprints@gmail.com
