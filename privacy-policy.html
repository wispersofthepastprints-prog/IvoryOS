Privacy Policy — IvoryOS

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Whispers of the Past ("we", "us") handles information in the IvoryOS app ("the app").

1. Information We Collect

  • Account and profile information: your email address, name, business name, phone number and location when you provide them.
  • Studio content: client names and contact details, bookings, packages, contract and email templates, invoices, payment status, Ledger settings, income and expense records.
  • Receipt images: photos or images you choose to attach to expense records.
  • Integration and purchase records: Google Calendar access credentials, Stripe Connect account identifiers, and subscription entitlement status.

2. Third-Party Integrations

  • Supabase: provides authentication, cloud database and receipt-image storage so your studio records are available to your account. Studio data is stored in the project's ap-southeast-2 (Sydney) region.
  • Google Calendar: with your permission, the app requests the calendar.events scope and creates events from new bookings. It does not import your existing events or provide conflict detection. Google access and refresh credentials are stored with your photographer profile; refresh operations occur on our Supabase Edge Functions.
  • Google Play Billing and RevenueCat: Google processes subscription payments. RevenueCat processes purchase and entitlement information so the app can determine whether Pro is active. We do not receive your full subscription payment-card details.
  • Stripe and Stripe Connect: client booking payments and photographer payouts are processed by Stripe. Payment information may include the booking ID, client email, AUD amount and connected-account identifier. We do not receive the client's full card details.

3. Data Storage and Security

Data is transmitted using HTTPS. Access to studio records is restricted by account and database access rules. Receipt images are stored in private Supabase Storage and viewed through short-lived signed URLs. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

4. Payments, Subscriptions and Cancellation

IvoryOS Pro subscriptions are billed by Google Play and auto-renew until cancelled. Manage or cancel in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Client booking payments are separate one-time Stripe transactions in AUD and are not subscription payments.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain studio data while your account is active. To request deletion of your account and associated data, email wispersofthepastprints@gmail.com from your registered address. We will process the request within 30 days, except where records must be retained for a legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention or dispute obligation. Cancelling a subscription does not delete your account.

6. Data Sharing and Children's Privacy

We do not sell your personal information or use your photographs to train AI models. Information is shared with the service providers listed above only as needed to operate the app, process payments, meet legal obligations or protect the service. IvoryOS is a business tool and is not directed at children under 16.

7. Changes to This Policy

We will update this page when the policy changes and revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes may also be communicated in the app or by email where practical.
