03Why this exists

I'm Geoff. I shoot weddings and portraits around Glen Innes, in the New England region of NSW. For years my "system" was a notebook, a spreadsheet, an inbox full of unsent contracts, and a quiet dread every time a couple asked "so… what happens next?"

The big studio apps wanted enterprise money for features I'd never touch. So I built the thing I actually needed — clients, bookings, contracts, questionnaires, invoices — and nothing I didn't.

IvoryOS runs my own business every day. If it ever stops being good enough for a real working photographer, I'll be the first to know — because it'll be my wedding season on the line.

— Geoff, wispers of the past
"The big studio apps felt like buying a truck to carry a camera bag. I wanted something built the way photographers actually work."
